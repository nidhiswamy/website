> A long standing log of all my hikes/runs

<h1>
  Apex and Enchanted Forest
</h1>
<h5>
Apex Park, Golden, CO
</h5>

5.4 miles.
1125 ft. elevation gain.
This hike allows bikes on odd days and hikers on even days of the month which is a great way to avoid the other when you're on the trail. It also has an equal amount of shade and sun, depending on the way you go.
<br/>

<h1>
  Lower Crater Lakes via South Boulder Trail
</h1>
<h5>James Peak Wilderness</h5>

6.6 miles.
1437 ft. elevation gain.
This is a moderately challenging but beautiful hike nestled in the James Peak Wilderness near Nederland, CO.
